> [!WARNING]
> Webhooks are experimental. Their configuration and event format may change. If a webhook does not work as expected, contact your Tiendu representative.

Configure one HTTPS callback URL and choose its events in **Ajustes → Tienda → Desarrollo y API → Webhooks** in the Merchant Center. Tiendu sends each event as a signed JSON `POST`. Recent attempts appear under **Entregas recientes** on the same page.

Continue with [events and payloads](events.md), [signature verification](signatures.md), or [delivery and retries](delivery.md).
