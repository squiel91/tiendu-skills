# Content

Manage storefront content and navigation from **Contenido** in the Merchant Center. Pages and blog posts hold the content; menus connect it to the storefront's navigation.

| Topic | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [Pages and blog posts](pages-and-blog.md) | Static pages, dated articles, content blocks, publication and alternate templates |
| [Menus](menus.md) | Navigation labels, destinations and ordering |

Publishing a page or post does not add it to a menu. Add its link to the menu the storefront uses. When changing a handle, keep the old link working with a redirect and review menu destinations.

## In the Merchant Center

- **Contenido → Páginas** manages static pages.
- **Contenido → Blog** manages blog posts.
- **Contenido → Menús** manages storefront navigation.

Each topic includes the fields and controls of its editor alongside the concept explanation.
